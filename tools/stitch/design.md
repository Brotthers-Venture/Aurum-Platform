# Aurum Platform — design system for Stitch

Institutional fiduciary software for fund administration, corporate services and investor portals.
The tone is a private bank, not a start-up: calm, precise, typographic. Avoid anything that looks
AI-generated or template-like (gradient blobs, glassmorphism, emoji, oversized illustrations,
"feature card" grids with icons in circles, purple gradients).

## Colour
- Ink #0F2340 — text, headers, dark surfaces. Navy is used sparingly.
- Accent #F8C618 — primary buttons and the hexagon mark only; text on it is ink.
- Metal #B8975A — thin rules, progress, small uppercase eyebrows.
- Canvas #F1EDE4 (warm cream) — page background. Surface #FFFFFF — cards.
- Neutrals are warm stone greys (#57534E, #A8A29E, #E7E5E0). No pure black, no cool greys.
- Status: positive #1F8A4C, warning #C9820F, critical #C0392B — always paired with a label.
- Workspace colours (used for small marks, never large fills): sales #C46A1E, PMO #8A4A9E,
  chat #1F8A84, data rooms #3558C0, billing #4F8A2B, compliance #9B2F3F.
- Few colours per screen. Colour carries meaning, not decoration.

## Type
- Headlines and key figures: Source Serif 4, regular weight, slight negative tracking.
- Interface and body: Hanken Grotesk, 12.5–14px; tabular numerals for all money.
- Eyebrows: 10.5–11px uppercase, 0.14–0.22em letter spacing, stone or metal colour.

## Shape and density
- Radius 8–14px on cards, 6–10px on inputs and buttons. Hairline borders (#E7E5E0), soft shadows.
- Dense, data-first layouts: tables with sticky headers, numbers right-aligned, small labels.
- Generous margins around dense content. One primary action per view.

## Brand mark
A gold hexagon made of four concentric hexagon bands. Wordmark "AURUM / ASSET MANAGEMENT" in a
serif. The platform is white-label: never bake the name into imagery; keep logo areas replaceable.

## Language
English only. Plain, specific, institutional wording ("Capital call 3 of 4 is due 15 December").
